What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?

10:41 AM done_all
verified
Official Statutory Fact Benchmark SID Verified

HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund comes with a mandatory statutory lock-in period of 3 years (36 months) from the date of allotment.

Each individual Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) installment carries its own independent 3-year lock-in cycle before becoming eligible for redemption or switch-out. Investments qualify for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961, up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year.

Lock-In Tenure 36 Months
Tax Treatment Sec 80C Deduction
Exit Load Nil (Post 3Y)
