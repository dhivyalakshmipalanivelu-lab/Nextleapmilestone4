## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calm, trustworthy, and friction-free aura of modern Indian wealth-tech platforms. Built specifically for a mutual fund and wealth intelligence assistant, it balances institutional fiscal credibility with conversational clarity and approachable warmth.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Modern retail investors, systematic investment plan (SIP) compounders, and first-time mutual fund buyers seeking clarity over financial jargon.
- **Emotional Intent:** Reassurance, transparency, steady growth, and unflinching precision. Users should feel in control of their capital without cognitive strain or panic during market volatility.

### Aesthetic Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Modern Fintech Minimalism** with **Subtle Tonal Layering**. It avoids over-saturated gamification or aggressive trade flashes, opting instead for calm white canvases, airy slate surfaces, crisp typographic structure, and purposeful emerald-mint accents signaling affirmative growth and verified data.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a high-clarity mint green, balanced by institutional slates and deep charcoals. Every color serves functional legibility and WCAG AAA/AA compliance.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#00D09C`):** The catalytic wealth green. Reserved for primary confirmation actions, positive returns (1D, 3Y, 5Y CAGR), active SIP indicators, and high-confidence AI insights.
- **Primary Pressed/Dark (`#00B386`):** State layer for active button presses and dark-contrast badge borders.
- **Primary Soft Tint (`#E6FAF5`):** Ambient background for affirmative status badges, positive return chips, and assistant message bubbles.
- **Secondary (`#475569`):** Slate for structural metadata, secondary actions, chart grid indicators, and fund manager profiles.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** High-purity white foundation for primary cards and root sheets.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, calm background tint for application shell, conversational rails, and module separations.
- **Neutral Raised Surface (`#F1F5F9`):** Subdued contrast backing for data table alternate rows, search bars, and segment pills.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep charcoal for core monetary sums, fund titles, and key conversational guidance.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Informational subtitles, NAV timestamps, expense ratios, and benchmark comparisons.
- **Border / Divider (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline structure that demarcates cards and table tiers without visual clutter.
- **Semantic Red / Drawdown (`#EF4444`):** Subdued coral-red reserved strictly for negative daily movement or portfolio exit flags, paired with soft backing (`#FEF2F2`).

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Inter**, leveraging its tabular numerals, optical balance, and legibility at dense financial data scales.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for total portfolio value, SIP return calculators, and top-level summary metrics. Tight letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) provides a modern editorial presence.
- **Headlines (MD/SM):** Utilized for fund category groups (e.g., Large Cap, Flexi Cap, Liquid Funds) and assistant recommendation titles.
- **Body:** Open line heights ensure extended fund prospectus notes, risk disclosures, and AI insights are scannable and comfortable.
- **Data & Numeric Display:** Numbers utilize Inter’s tabular lining features (`tnum`, `cv05`) to maintain consistent horizontal alignment across asset allocation tables, NAV trackers, and annualized percentages.
- **Micro Labels:** Used for asset categorization (Debt, Equity, Hybrid), expense ratio tags, and SEBI-mandated riskometer indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

A strict 8-point base grid governs layout density, element grouping, and responsive layout scaling.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column fluid stream with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom navigation holds thumb-friendly financial actions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem`, allowing side-by-side fund comparison cards and persistent assistant drawers.
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (max width: 1240px) centered within an ambient `#F8FAFC` viewport, featuring an asymmetrical split: 7 columns for portfolio analytics and fund metrics, 5 columns for the interactive conversational wealth assistant.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Reserved for metric-to-label grouping, badge inner padding, and currency symbol offsets.
- **Component Spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Internal padding for mutual fund cards, conversational bubbles, and input fields.
- **Section Spacing (`space-xl`):** Gap between major functional groups (e.g., SIP installments due vs. Recommended Funds).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Soft Ambient Depth** combined with crisp structural borders, eliminating heavy drop shadows in favor of a clean, institutional airiness.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F8FAFC` base application surface.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#FFFFFF` background bound by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Shadow is extremely faint: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Selected Fund):** `#FFFFFF` background with border tint shifting to `#CBD5E1` and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Assistant Bottom Sheet / Modal Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` background elevated with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, supported by a soft slate-tinted backdrop blur (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).
- **Surface Nesting:** Embedded sub-sections within cards (such as asset distribution bars or historical return matrices) employ `#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9` surfaces with no border and zero elevation.

## Shapes

The geometric identity relies on uniform, friendly curves that convey approachability without appearing toy-like.

### Application Rules
- **Container Cards & Dashboards:** Utilize default `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) corners, delivering the signature soft-square finish prominent in contemporary wealth management apps.
- **Buttons & Search Inputs:** Styled with `rounded-md` (8px / 0.5rem) to preserve clean tactile utility and distinct boundaries.
- **Filter Tags & Return Badges:** Styled as pills using fully rounded edges (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for fast visual separation from rectangular data cards.
- **Modal Dialogs & Bottom Drawers:** Top edges cut to 24px (`rounded-t-2xl`) on mobile devices to create an inviting pull-sheet aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Buttons ("Start SIP", "Invest Now"):** Solid `#00D09C` background with bold `#0F172A` text (maximizing contrast and readability), 44px minimum height, 16px horizontal padding, and a subtle active press compression (`scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary Buttons ("View Holdings", "Compare NAV"):** Clean white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` label, shifting to `#F1F5F9` on hover.
- **Assistant Prompt Action Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#F1F5F9` base with `#475569` text, transitioning to `#E6FAF5` border with `#00A87E` text on tap.

### Mutual Fund Summary Cards
- Master container: 16px radius, pure white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` outline, 16px internal padding.
- Upper tier: Fund house avatar (36x36px rounded-md), full fund scheme title in `label-lg`, and category badge (e.g., "Equity • Mid Cap").
- Lower tier: 3-column metric display displaying "Min SIP", "3Y Returns" (highlighted in `#00D09C` bold), and "Rating" (stars or score badge).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Positive Return Badge:** Background `#E6FAF5`, text `#008763`, format: `▲ 18.4% (3Y)`.
- **Negative / Neutral Return Badge:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#DC2626` for drawdowns; `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569` for index benchmarks.
- **Riskometer Gauge Pill:** Low / Moderate / Very High risk displayed in a 20px tall pill with light neutral backing and colored status dot.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Amount & Search Fields:** Minimum height 48px, `#FFFFFF` interior, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `rounded-md` (8px). Fixed `₹` symbol prefix anchored in deep slate `#0F172A`.
- **Interactive SIP Range Slider:** Thin 4px `#E2E8F0` track, active fill `#00D09C`, accompanied by a 20x20px pure white thumb with a crisp `#00D09C` border and subtle ambient shadow.

### Conversational AI Assistant Modules
- **Assistant Message Bubbles:** Outlined in 1px `#E2E8F0`, filled with `#F8FAFC`, featuring `rounded-lg` corners with an asymmetric sharp bottom-left corner (4px) to indicate conversational origin.
- **User Message Bubbles:** Filled with deep charcoal `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-lg` with sharp bottom-right corner.
- **Inline Action Cards:** Interactive mini-cards inside assistant responses that show direct investment paths with inline "Invest ₹500" one-click action buttons.

### Checkboxes & Segment Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 20x20px square with 4px border radius. In active state, filled with `#00D09C` containing an optic-white checkmark.
- **Segmented Time Tabs (1M, 6M, 1Y, 3Y, All):** Housed in a `#F1F5F9` pill container; active tab is elevated `#FFFFFF` with 4px radius and bold `#0F172A` typography.
